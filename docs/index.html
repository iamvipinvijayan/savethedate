How to Reach

Open in Google Maps
🚗

By Car

Head to Parambil Bazar, Calicut. Alba Arcade Auditorium is right in the heart of the bazar area. Parking is available at the venue.

🚆

From Railway Station

Kozhikode Railway Station is approximately 7 km from the venue.

🛺 Auto: Autos are available right outside the station. Ask for Alba Arcade, Parambil Bazar.
🚌 Private Bus: Board any bus headed towards Parambil Bazar from the station road and alight at Parambil Bazar stop.

🚌

From KSRTC Bus Stand

KSRTC Stand is on Mavoor Road, Kozhikode.

🛺 Auto: Autos are available outside the stand. Ask for Alba Arcade, Parambil Bazar.
🚌 Private Bus: Board a private bus from Mavoor Road towards Parambil Bazar and alight at Parambil Bazar stop.

🏢

From Private Bus Stand

From Palayam or Mofussil Bus Stand, Kozhikode.

🛺 Auto: Autos are readily available. Ask for Alba Arcade, Parambil Bazar.
🚌 Private Bus: Board any bus passing through Parambil Bazar and alight at Parambil Bazar stop.

📍 Landmark: Alba Arcade Auditorium, Parambil Bazar, Calicut — near the Parambil Bazar junction. Look for the Alba Arcade building.
